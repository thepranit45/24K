Our Story

More Than Just a Haircut

Founded in the heart of Sangamner, 24 K Hair Studio was born from a single belief: every client deserves to look and feel their absolute best. What started as a vision for bespoke grooming has grown into Sangamner's most trusted luxury hair studio.

Our philosophy is simple — premium service, precision cuts, and an experience that sends you out looking sharp and feeling confident. Every appointment is treated with the same level of care and attention, whether it's a classic trim or a complete grooming session.
